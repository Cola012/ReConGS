The idea

Structural knowledge.
Dependable visual evidence.

Text-to-3D generation with pretrained video and reconstruction models requires structurally compatible features and dependable visual evidence. Feature-value alignment alone leaves cross-view relationships insufficiently constrained, while inconsistent generated details can induce unsupported geometric corrections.

ReConGS addresses both issues. Cross-view feature coupling combines F3D interaction, feature–relation distillation, and adaptive low-rank adaptation of a pretrained reconstruction tail. Reliability-aware refinement retains view-specific evidence and uses agreement, support, and directional coverage to control appearance and geometry corrections, with stricter control for geometry. The result is an explicit 3D Gaussian asset without test-time gradient optimization.

Method

From text to a refined 3D asset

A shared video generator, a pretrained reconstruction decoder, and three learned forward updates.

Text prompt“A ripe pineapple”

    Generate shared evidence

    Wan produces nine multi-view references. A readout of the same final latent supplies view-indexed hidden features.

    9 views + hidden features
  2. 02

    Couple and reconstruct

    F3D couples cross-view features; stitching connects them to the adapted AnySplat tail for initial reconstruction.

    Initial Gaussians + cameras
  3. 03

    Refine with reliability

    View agreement, effective support, and directional coverage gate bounded appearance and geometry updates.

Human evaluation

A blinded pairwise study

50 adult volunteers evaluated assets from 30 T3Bench prompts. Method identities were hidden, and presentation order and left/right positions were randomized.

Each participant assessed 30 pairs—ten per baseline—independently for surface quality, cross-view consistency, and text alignment.
